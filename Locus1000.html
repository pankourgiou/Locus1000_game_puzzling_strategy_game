Locus — puzzle mode

Same idea as the original duel, scaled up to a 20×20 board: every piece's legal moves come from its own equation. The Core moves like a king and must be captured — and unlike a static target, it actively flees one step away from your pieces every time you miss, so the board keeps shifting under you. Shields shuffle too. Your job each round is to spot the one piece whose equation actually reaches it — right now.

Every puzzle rolls fresh weights into each piece's equation (a different radius, a different constant), so the same shape never solves the same way twice — read the numbers, don't rely on memory. You get a limited number of moves to find the capture, and one minute per move — a wrong move or a stalled clock both cost you a move.
